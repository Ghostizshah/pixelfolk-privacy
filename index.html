Pixelfolk Farm — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026 · App: Pixelfolk Farm (Android) · Package: com.ghostizshah.pixelfolkfarm

The short version

Pixelfolk Farm collects no data. None. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no in-app purchases, and the game does not use the internet.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Your save game

Your farm's progress is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. We have no server and the app has no network access, so we cannot see it or retrieve it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The game includes a Copy Save Code button, which puts a code representing your save on your device's clipboard so you can move a farm to another device yourself. That code goes only where you paste it — it is never sent to us. As with anything you copy, other apps on your device may be able to read the clipboard.

The matching Paste button reads whatever is currently on your clipboard, so you can drop a save code into the box without typing it. It is used only to fill that box: nothing from your clipboard is stored, kept, or sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or network identity.

Two small things the game does on your device, for completeness. It reads your battery level to show the battery figure in its header — that number is displayed and then discarded, never stored or sent. And the Rate This Game button hands a Play Store link to your device to open in the Play Store app; the game itself still has no internet access of its own.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes any of the above (for example, adding optional ads), this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly noted in the app's store listing.
